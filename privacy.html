Privacy Policy

My YouTube Tools is a personal set of scripts used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channels it manages. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the tools access

After I sign in with my own Google account, the tools use the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API to:

  • upload videos to my channels and update their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and privacy settings;
  • list the videos on my channels;
  • read my channels' own analytics reports (views, impressions and click-through rates).

Where the data goes

Everything stays on my own computer. The sign-in token and any reports are stored locally and are not sent to any other server, sold, or shared with anyone. The tools do not collect any data about viewers or other YouTube users beyond the aggregate channel analytics that YouTube provides to channel owners.

Google API Services

The tools' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. They also use YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
